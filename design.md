# Jobvion design system

A warm paper canvas, deep pine ink and bright mint are the Jobvion palette. The compact Jobvion mark combines a J with an ascending check. Funnel Display and Funnel Sans carry the public site and workspace. Rounded rectangular controls use a strong pine primary action and outline secondary actions.

The homepage starts with the product promise and a real example CV beside its computed checklist result. Workflow, ATS check, template examples, pricing and common questions share a single page. The page uses product outputs as evidence. Figures derive from working features or clearly labeled examples.

Separate editorial and auth pages share the header, palette and spacing. Use the device color scheme on first visit and respect a visitor’s manual toggle. The CV remains white paper in both modes.
